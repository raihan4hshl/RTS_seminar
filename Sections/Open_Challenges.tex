\section{Conclusion}
In this chapter, we have explored the challenges of resource sharing in real-time systems, particularly focusing on priority inversion and deadlocks. We discussed how these issues can lead to significant operational problems, including missed deadlines and system failures. The chapter also provided an overview of classic protocols designed to manage resource sharing effectively, such as Non-Preemptive Protocol (NPP), Priority Inheritance Protocol (PIP), Priority Ceiling Protocol (PCP), and Stack Resource Policy (SRP). Each protocol has its own benefits and drawbacks, making them suitable for different scenarios in real-time systems.
The discussion highlighted the importance of selecting the appropriate protocol based on the specific requirements and constraints of the system. By understanding these protocols, developers can better design real-time systems that are robust against resource sharing issues, ensuring timely and reliable operation.
